## 2. The job

Build everything in `docs/prototype-brief.md` under "Must have", with its tests, so that "Done means" is true. That brief is the contract. The story bible, phase-two, challenger-progression, and art-direction docs are the design. Where they conflict with GDD.md or CONFIG.md, they win, and you update GDD.md and CONFIG.md to match (mark changed rows [Proposed] for Nicoh to confirm).

The heart of the game, so you keep priorities straight when you have to cut:
- You play a tall, lonely, immortal cat whose kit never changes, except for its Phase Two transformation at 50% HP each fight (refill, faster, a different magic-based move set).
- One small cat keeps coming back over nine lives, stronger each time, in a new outfit with a new skill. The agent picks which life comes next and how it fights, between fights only.
- The fights turn into a bond. Dialogue deepens by tier, challengers start to hesitate, life 8 doesn't fight, life 9 always wins, and losing to it should feel like letting go. Story moves gradually through light, music, props, and behavior. Nothing abrupt.
- Any challenger from life 2 on can kill the boss (the short "too soon" ending).

If time runs short, cut in this order: thief decoy, knight, tier props, music layers beyond the drone, then the live Worker (keep scripted and heuristic planners). Never cut Phase Two, the progression curve, hesitation, life 8, life 9, or the endings.
