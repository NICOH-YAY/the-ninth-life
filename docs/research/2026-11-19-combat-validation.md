# Royal combat behavior and validation — 19 November 2026

Automatic late-life weapon lowering no longer triggers just because the player has not attacked for four seconds. Guarding, moving and jumping keep a normal hostile encounter active. Existing deliberately authored hesitation remains; it is not a universal seven-second ceasefire.

Non-tutorial challengers recover from hitstun into an attack with the normal 0.30-second contact windup. For the next 0.9 seconds, light Cleave hits still damage them and show hit flashes, but apply reduced knockback and do not repeatedly cancel the counter. Their counter does not abandon itself to another light-attack dodge tell. Heavy attacks still interrupt, the resistance expires, and the tutorial remains interruptible. Player jump, guard and dash remain available answers. No actor receives health/damage bonuses for autoplay.

Each visitor now has independent weapon geometry and timing in `src/core/enemy-patterns.ts`: a readable tutorial wave; short hooked blade pair; returning chain flail; airborne chakram fan; aimed star needles; heavy low shield shock; flank blink with high/low daggers; alternating low/high frost spears; and converging broken-sword arcs with a ground wave. Projectile `style` is a LifeId for rendering; optional `radius`, `low`, `gravity` and `returnAtDistance` describe real motion/collision behavior. The returning flail reverses toward its owner; low attacks reward jumping, and the high frost lane catches an airborne king while passing over a grounded king.

Kneel uses the existing internal `reap` move ID and damage value. Its tell snapshots the visitor's horizontal position. At active start it strikes that point once, rather than firing a traveling blade or following the visitor. Base radius/height are 88/72 pixels and transformed radius/height are 126/120. A target outside the radius or above the height avoids the seal. A damaged target is grounded immediately, loses upward velocity and pending double-jump, and kneels for 0.42 seconds (0.65 transformed). Defensive invulnerability, decoys and armor continue to prevent damage and forced kneeling. `ROYAL_GRAVITY` exports shared geometry; `ch.kneelT` exposes the bounded reaction for presentation. Longer authored tells preserve the double-jump counter and leave the target readable.

Targeted presentation fixes replaced two arrival steps per rendered frame with a delta accumulator and drove melee anticipation from `windupT` followed by `attackFlashT`. Initial browser assertions measured exactly 30 simulation ticks and identical challenger position after half a second at 30, 60 and 120 Hz, both legacy shield mirror/offset directions and an anticipation frame of 1 at a 0.15-second windup, with zero runtime errors. The later circular royal aegis uses centered artwork and facing mirroring; final QA should verify that contract instead of the legacy side offset.

The deterministic suite passes 123 tests across 13 files, including 12 new behavioral tests for retaliation, visitor patterns and gravity. Main and worker strict TypeScript checks passed. The hesitation damage fixture now tests an actual Cleave during a known hesitation, avoiding a probabilistic search for a conveniently timed hit. The victory fixture now uses the ordinary aggressive bot for all nine encounters, replacing a final-life Reap-only exploit that no longer matches the move. Both scripted and heuristic full headless routes reach Victory; the final encounter leaves approximately 5.5% minimum effective king health for seed 42. No game balance values were relaxed to obtain the result.

`scripts/royal-routes.mjs` verifies production-browser Normal, Do Nothing and Victory routes from life one through ordinary policy inputs, without debug jumps or actor/stat overrides. It checks final lethal outcomes, all nine distinct defeated visitors for Victory, the ruined-castle coda for losses, the surviving king/fallen visitor for Victory, and return to menu. All three production-browser routes passed cleanly from life one: Do Nothing in 29 seconds, Normal in 42 seconds, Victory in 42 seconds.
