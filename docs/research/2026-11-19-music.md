## Runtime mix

All runtime files are full-length MP3, stereo, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps. FFmpeg normalized them toward -20 LUFS / -2 dBTP, with a 0.6 s fade-in and 2 s fade-out. Originals remain untouched. The runtime payload is 9,946,303 bytes. Each file passed a complete FFmpeg decode, and FFprobe verified durations and channels.

Start playback from the existing user gesture, honor mute across every cue, and stop music when leaving the game. Suggested HTMLAudio volumes before the existing master: years 0.48, battle 0.60, aftermath 0.50. These are starting points for an in-game listening pass, not measured final mix approval. Avoid playing procedural background drones over the score.

Crossfade scenes over about 2.5 s rather than restarting a song with every dialogue line. Preserve the years track's position during brief menu overlays. These are composed pieces, **not seamless game loops**; repeat with a 2.5 s overlap or accept a short musical breath at the boundary. Finale should not loop. No exact beat-drop synchronization has been claimed; the battle arrangement builds across the recording.

Keep an independent voice gain bus. While dialogue or future voiceovers are active, lower music to roughly 28% of its normal scene volume, with a 0.15 s duck and 0.8 s recovery. Keep attack telegraphs audible under the battle music. Dialogue portraits and optional future voice files should not dictate soundtrack reloads. Voice assets can later use a speaker/line-ID keyed lookup; no voice recordings were generated or bundled here.
