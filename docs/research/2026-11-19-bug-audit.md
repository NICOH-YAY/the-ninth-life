# Bug audit — 2026-11-19

Scope: read-only review of the mobile-combat implementation before the current story/combat redesign. Existing suite: 111 tests passed across 10 files. Line references below describe the reviewed version and may move during the redesign.

## Confirmed findings

1. **P1 — Late challengers repeatedly stop fighting.** `src/core/fight.ts:867`–`879` lowers weapons on life six onward after four seconds without a player attack. Moving, jumping, dashing and blocking do not reset this clock. Lowered state can last seven seconds and recur. This explains passive opponents while the player experiments with movement. Trigger this only through a deliberate narrative/choice state; normal enemies should keep attacking while the player defends.

2. **P2 — Guard art never follows facing.** `src/game/scenes/FightScene.ts:281` positions the asymmetric guard atlas without flipping it or applying a facing offset. `damageBoss` in `src/core/fight.ts:778` guards damage from either side, so the reported directional failure is visual, not a directional damage check. Flip the shield and position it on the defended side; apply the same rule to challenger shields. Verify both left-facing and right-facing cases.

3. **P2 — Arrival movement depends on render frame rate.** `src/game/scenes/FightScene.ts:225` advances the simulator up to twice per rendered frame during arrival, unlike combat fixed-step accumulation. Entrances therefore move twice as fast at 120 Hz as at 60 Hz. Use a delta-based accumulator or an explicitly timed staged arrival animation.

4. **P2 — Enemy attack poses do not align with melee contact.** The render block of `src/game/scenes/FightScene.ts` selects cleave animation throughout the attack state but ordinarily chooses frames from global time (`floor(t * 10) % 6`). Core contact is instead determined by `windupT` in `src/core/fight.ts:915`–`936`. A challenger can visibly swipe before the damaging contact. Drive anticipation and strike frames from windup/attack-flash progress, then transition to a recovery clock.

5. **P2 — Hit reactions can keep enemies retreating under repeated light attacks.** The common hitstun state transitions to backoff, then waits until reaching its preferred distance and making another probabilistic decision. Repeated light attacks plus knockback can restart this retreat cycle before a retaliatory strike. Give stronger challengers a bounded response plan, limited stagger resistance or an explicitly telegraphed counter, retaining a fair dodge/block window.

## Art/behavior findings tied to the request

- `src/core/fight.ts:807`–`832` provides only shared wave/orb ability geometry with small per-archetype extras. Distinct visual identities currently exceed mechanical distinctions. Author explicit independent ability schedules, ranges and counters per challenger.
- `src/game/scenes/TitleScene.ts:17` applies an infinite 24-second scale tween to the generated castle image. The subtle image zoom matches the reported unnatural main-menu motion. A static composed silhouette with restrained native particles is appropriate.
- Existing actor rendering swaps legacy 48×72 idle/run sprites and 144×144 ability sprites. Their anchors differ (`71/72` and `110/144`) and the import pipeline detects feet from the last opaque central pixel. This is a plausible source of floating/size changes, but was not visually quantified in this audit. Treat it as a verification task, not a confirmed root cause: inspect every grounded frame for paw contact at FLOOR, including entrance/run and transitions into attack.

## Verification gaps

The passing logic tests do not prove shield facing, grounded rendered feet, presentation contact timing or 30/60/120 Hz entrance equivalence. Add focused browser assertions for these behaviors after the redesign settles; avoid screenshot-only checks that cannot establish foot contact. Inspect menu and HUD at 16:9, ultrawide and portrait sizes for intentional letterboxing, legible controls and clipping. The Phaser canvas already uses FIT and centered 960×540 geometry, so there is no confirmed aspect-ratio defect from configuration alone.
