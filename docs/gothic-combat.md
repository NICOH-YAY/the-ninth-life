# The Ninth Life: gothic revision
This is the latest contract. It supersedes the single-choice conversations, prior three-track score, former menu silhouette, AI-painted arena, and previous royal attack behavior described in docs/royal-revision.md. The canonical project remains D:/Coding VS/the-ninth-life.

Each anonymous visitor has two required branching dialogue exchanges before combat. Each choice receives a spoken king line and visitor response; the second question and choices reflect the first answer and existing bond. Nine conversations use different objects, memories and motives. Shared broken-crown accessories connect otherwise distinct outfits and weapons to the king.

The base king is a crowned sword fighter: three-step Crownsteel cuts/rise/thrust, an Oathpiercer lunge, bounded Kneel gravity, and advancing King's Procession echoes. The transformed monster has a larger, multi-armed spectral silhouette and separate geometry: paired Rend claws, a nonhoming Hollow Choir fang fan, three staggered Grave Hands eruptions, and expanding Eclipse contact. Monster feet remain grounded. Fixed-step combat stays deterministic.

Visitors retain independent ranged patterns and also use individual contact ranges, tells and reactions. The hooked blade pulls, chain flail presses guard, acrobat recoils into the air, astral strike lifts, warhammer launches and breaks guard pressure, mirror daggers strike quickly, frost spear reaches farther, and broken royal sword drives the king back. Sprite recoil, blood, hitstop, and real knockback accompany damage; missed swings do not imply hits.

The new environments and distant-castle menu are composed from Ansimuz's licensed hand-drawn CC0 tiles and layers at a fixed 480x270 grid with exact2x display. Sources and composition are recorded under assets/gothic-v5. Higgsfield provides original king, monster, coronation, visitor idle/hurt, and effect sheets. Ozzbit's animation template informs motion principles; paid template files are not copied. Uniform feet and source scales are retained.

The coronation physically reaches for, lifts, holds and dons the crown, then transforms the victor. Defeat is a single white-gold sword thrust through the heart; the challenger holds the blade through the wounded king's last words and decree explosion. The blade is not withdrawn, and both vanish into ruined dawn. Victory still leaves the king alive after killing the last incarnation.

There are twelve independent Scott Buckley CC BY4.0 score cues: Incantation for menu, Hiraeth for passing years, Call To Adventure/Juggernaut/Legionnaire2022/Race The Sun/The Summoning/Goliath/Honour Among Thieves/Snowfall/Vanguard for the nine visitors, and Helios for aftermath. Music is lazy loaded, remembers cue position, crossfades, and ducks to24% during dialogue. The user selected closer licensed/original music instead of unlicensed commercial reference recordings.

Processed Chequered Ink Foley supplies sword, chain, metal guard, wood, ice, flesh/stab, footstep and dash sounds. Full original archives remain ignored local evidence; selected edited runtime sounds are integrated into the game with clear provenance, not redistributed as a standalone raw asset pack.

Normal, Do Nothing and Victory remain consequences of ordinary player inputs. No forced final loss or autoplay-only health/damage bonuses are introduced. Publishing remains the existing prototype branch/GitHub Pages workflow; main is not merged or pushed.
