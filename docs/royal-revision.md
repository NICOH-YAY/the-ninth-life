# The crown remembers: current contract
This later user-directed contract supersedes friendly departures, named visitors, passive late lives and procedural-only music in docs/mobile-combat.md and earlier documents.

The player remains mobile across the entire hall. Visitors are different embodiments of the same returning soul: their readable names are lost, and speech/HUD uses unreadable marks. Internal life IDs remain stable for saved summaries and planner schemas. Every arrival offers an explicit vulnerable or guarded reply; choices change bond and subsequent encounter planning. Required portrait reading time remains intact.

Royal abilities have a shared purpose: Royal Sentence, Crown Recall, Kneel and Last Decree. Kneel marks the target's ground position before exerting gravity; movement and the specialist double jump provide counters. Ascension strengthens its seal. Visitors have separate authored projectile trajectories, tells, cooldowns and weapon colors. The former raincoat/umbrella appearance is replaced with an ochre chain warden; the internal umbrella counter ID remains a compatibility alias.

Repeated light hits cannot indefinitely cancel retaliation. Heavy royal moves still interrupt. Late encounters no longer repeatedly enter a seven-second passive state after a few seconds without a player attack. Entrances use the fixed simulation timestep, independent of render frame rate. Shield art follows facing.

Normal and Do Nothing defeat are lethal. The returning visitor kills the king, whose last nova destroys the hall and kingdom; sunlight enters the ruins. Victory leaves the king alone after killing the final incarnation. No ending has a friendly departure. Ending results still follow actual combat or deliberate inactivity rather than a forced last-life victory.

The distant castle menu is authored on a fixed 320x180 pixel grid and does not zoom or drift. Arena variants are exported to 320x180 with a 32-color limit and displayed with nearest-neighbor sampling. New visitor walk/combat frames share explicit feet at (72,110) inside 144px cells; weapon effects and blood use independent sprite atlases.

Music is licensed human-composed material by Kevin MacLeod: At Rest for the castle and centuries, Five Armies for combat, Virtutes Vocis for the aftermath. The tracks are CC BY 4.0 and credited in-game and ATTRIBUTION.md; originals, license evidence and processing details are in assets/audio/manifest.json. They are emotional orchestral/choir alternatives, not the referenced commercial recordings. Scene music crossfades over2.5s, dialogue ducks it to28%, and M mutes music, voices and effects. Full compositions include composed pauses at loop boundaries. Optional voice cues are registered in src/game/voice-cues.ts; no recorded acting is supplied yet.

Phaser remains the engine. A Godot migration would require a separate tested port and would not resolve asset consistency or choreography automatically. No new hosting provider is introduced; publication uses the existing GitHub Pages workflow on prototype/first-playable.
