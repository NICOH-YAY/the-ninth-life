# Court audio v8

The existing twelve licensed Scott Buckley cues remain separate. Quiet waiting uses a restrained mix. Higher tension swells the score, while anticipation and brief impact breaths make room for contact; perfect blocks, poise breaks, phase changes and climaxes can trigger one bounded original orchestral accent voice. The accent is a deterministic original synthesis, not a commercial reference recording or an unrelated full-song layer. Perfect-block and poise-break Foley are isolated Chequered Ink sword-clash and metal-clang edits, at original pitch. Existing game-use license and credits remain applicable. Source recording archives are not redistributed.

Run node assets/court-v8/process-audio.mjs to reproduce these three OGG assets. audio-processing.json records sources, processing and hashes.
